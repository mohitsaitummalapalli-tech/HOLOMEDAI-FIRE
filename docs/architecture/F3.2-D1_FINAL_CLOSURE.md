This is the minimal necessary contract extension.
F1 produces a trajectory which F3 converts into a `SweptCutSurface`. The cutter operations are represented by `SliceOperation` which is passed into the F2 `SliceEngine`. F3.2-A's contract specifies `SweptCutSurface` as a standalone dataclass that does not inherit from `CutSurface` (to avoid circular coupling/inheritance in F3.2-A). 
Therefore, `SliceOperation.cut_surface` must mathematically and legally represent either a planar or swept cut without introducing runtime assertions or breaking `PlanarCutSurface` initialization. A typing union is the exact structural pattern to achieve this static type guarantee without altering any runtime serialization, construction, or authority metadata.
