## 1. Global Partition Assignment

Disconnected triangulated patches are assigned to child meshes (A or B) using a deterministic connectivity-based dual-graph traversal. 
- The target mesh is split into individual sub-triangles by the intersection boundaries.
- The intersection boundaries are added as bidirectional explicit edges in the adjacency graph.
- A deterministic BFS (seeded by sequentially increasing triangle index) identifies connected components. The boundaries act as topological barriers.
- **Child A/B Labeling**: The resulting components are sorted by comparing their lexicographical minimum vertex coordinate. This guarantees perfectly invariant labeling (Child A = geometrically smallest bound component) regardless of arbitrary index arrays or centroid-of-mass shifts that might fail for asymmetric/concave cuts. Centroids were explicitly removed in favor of this rigorous invariant vertex comparison.

## 2. Exact Supported Topology

- **One Separating Loop**: SUPPORTED. Produces exactly 2 child pieces.
- **Multiple Disjoint Loops**: SUPPORTED if they partition the mesh into exactly 2 connected components.
- **Nested Loops**: FAIL-CLOSED (would create 3+ partitions, rejected).
- **Self-Intersecting Loops**: FAIL-CLOSED (`BoundaryValidationError: Branched/Ambiguous`).
- **Disconnected Target Components**: FAIL-CLOSED.
- **Boundary Touching Target Topology**: SUPPORTED via strict point welding and localized edge-on-boundary traversal.

## 3. Loop -> Mesh Boundary Consistency

- Every intersection segment acts as an immutable topological barrier in the modified mesh. 
- The modified mesh strictly incorporates the exact boundary segments; zero-length and duplicate edges are strictly filtered out during `BoundaryGraph` and `TargetMeshPartitioner` initialization.
- D3 outputs are explicitly open surfaces; capping (D4) is not performed.

## 4. Local Triangle Subdivision

- Triangle division operates locally on individual target triangles.
- The boundary chain is embedded into the local 2D projection utilizing the single target triangle's explicit mathematical normal.
- Retriangulation executes standard robust Ear-Clipping on the left/right simple polygons induced by the internal chain. 
- Positive area is explicitly verified; zero-area degenerate triangles are avoided.
