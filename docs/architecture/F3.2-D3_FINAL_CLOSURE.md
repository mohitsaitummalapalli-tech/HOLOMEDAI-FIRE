## 1. Global Partition Assignment

Disconnected triangulated patches are assigned to child meshes (A or B) using a deterministic connectivity-based dual-graph traversal. 
- The target mesh is split into individual sub-triangles by the intersection boundaries.
- The intersection boundaries are added as bidirectional explicit edges in the adjacency graph.
- A deterministic BFS (seeded by sequentially increasing triangle index) identifies connected components. The boundaries act as topological barriers.
- **Child A/B Labeling**: The resulting components are sorted by comparing their lexicographical minimum vertex coordinate. This guarantees perfectly invariant labeling (Child A = geometrically smallest bound component) regardless of arbitrary index arrays or centroid-of-mass shifts that might fail for asymmetric/concave cuts. Centroids were explicitly removed in favor of this rigorous invariant vertex comparison.
